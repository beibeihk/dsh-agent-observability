# ADR-003: Deterministic detectors before judges

Status: accepted.

V1 needs reproducible, keyless labels with local evidence and defensible false-positive boundaries. Deterministic thresholds meet that need and can be evaluated against predeclared synthetic cases. LLM judges would introduce model dependence, cost and content exposure without a task-state ground truth. They remain a future opt-in exporter/adapter extension. No total reliability score is introduced, and universal progress/answer-correctness detection is deferred.
